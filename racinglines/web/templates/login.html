What is this?

Sportsbook pricing for all racing disciplines

Fair odds for race sports, checked against the markets.

racinglines models every driver's and rider's chances from race data, reads the prediction markets alongside, and turns both into a fair price for each outcome. Use it to set your own motorsport lines, to check the lines your sportsbook provider sends you, or to offer markets your feed doesn't carry yet.

Talk to us

Books that set their own lines

An independent fair price for every motorsport market you list, next to the exchanges' prices, so you can see where your line has drifted before your customers do.

Books that run on a provider's feed

A second opinion on the motorsport lines your platform provider sends, and prices for the head-to-heads, top-N finishes and futures its feed leaves out.

Platform providers and resellers

A motorsport pricing module you can offer to every operator you serve, without building a race model of your own.
